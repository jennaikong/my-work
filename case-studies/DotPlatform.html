Dot Platform

How do we build something of value that leverages a new emerging technology?

The Problem

People in the US do not take advantage of their healthcare benefits

They miss opportunities to prevent small health issues from becoming large ones. As a result, people may end up developing complex health conditions, leaving them financially devastated.

At Included Health, we are positioned to help US employers make healthcare accessible to their employees.

How might we use AI to scale our ability to raise healthcare literacy and access for millions of US citizens?
The Solution

A smart, empathetic and personalized healthcare guide

One who can help members answer coverage questions, direct healthcare concerns appropriately, and even route people to the right care immediately.

This problem and solution is the backbone of our Dot platform program.

My Role

Leading design from end to end

As a staff designer, I was responsible for the strategy, the principles, the patterns and the process for developing an agentic AI healthcare assistant.

I started with discovery workshops and ideation sessions, then took prioritized ideas forward to build our flows and craft designs.

In doing this, I was able to
  1. 01Align stakeholders on the problem worth solving
  2. 02Generate over 320 ideas to push creative thinking
  3. 03Be the voice of the user during the prioritization of ideas
  4. 04Create non-deterministic flows to support an AI-based feature
  5. 05Design patterns for AI features, establish visual identity, and shape voice and tone to create an empathetic and beloved chatbot
