Building AI Design Culture

How do we help designers think through product development using AI as a core design material?

The Challenge

A team moving fast without a map

When GenAI took off in 2023, our teams moved fast but without a shared foundation. AI features shipped reactively — shaped by what the technology could do, not what users needed. We didn't yet know how to design for non-deterministic flows, which led to rework and churn. Meanwhile, the gap between engineering and design kept widening, breeding mistrust and siloed work.

"Designers weren't lacking talent or ambition — they lacked a framework."

A survey I ran across the design org confirmed it: designers needed structure to evaluate AI's fit and to advocate for users whenever they were in rooms where engineering and product set the agenda.

The Approach

Three ways I shaped the culture

01

Hackathons

Cross-functional hackathons where engineering, product and design built together. Designers went from polishing ideas to prototyping them.

02

AI Design Principles

Values, principles and a systematic way of constructing AI-UX patterns that gave every AI experience we shipped a shared, user-centered standard.

03

Innovation Workshop

Bringing it all together, I co-led a workshop to give designers a framework to understand how to think about AI features and build an AI-based product that can meet user's needs, is feasible for engineering and aligns to the company's value system.
