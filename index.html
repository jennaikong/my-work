About Me

A design leader who turns ambiguity into
strategy, alignment
& products that matter.

I've spent over a decade leading design at the intersection of emergent technology and healthcare — and what sets me apart is the ability to operate at every altitude. I can sit in a boardroom aligning executives on a product vision, then turn around and coach a designer through a tough research synthesis.

At Included Health, I've defined the AI design strategy for our innovation team, led the vision and execution of a multi-agent healthcare assistant, and established the design principles and patterns that now guide how our company builds AI. I believe that great design leadership means building the conditions for great work — clear direction, strong culture, and the psychological safety for teams to take creative risks.

When I'm not leading design, you might find me jamming on the drums, playing pick-up volleyball, and painting with my kids.
