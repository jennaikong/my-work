About Me

A design leader who embraces ambiguity with
curiosity, a can-do attitude
& an ego-free mindset.

Words often to describe me: "out-of-the-box thinker", "a pioneer", and "thoughtful about the details". This has led me to believe that my edge as a design leader is someone who gives a shit and will do whatever it takes to ensure the end product makes a positive difference for the user. My process is rooted in empathy, collaboration, scalability and growth.

With a product design background spanning mobile apps, web platforms, and internal tools, I bring a systems-design thinking, research-led approach to every project. I believe the best design is the kind users never have to think throughout their entire journey.

When I'm not designing, you might find me playing the drums, playing pick-up volleyball, and painting with my kids.
